role across acts, the search page, a cited MCP answer. Mode: Read. Build path: code-led.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The law is fixed terrain, drawn the same for everyone; the reader's role is a purple
course over it, control to control, each control an article with its citation. It refuses the
legal database's sidebar tree over a wall of text, and the EU portal.

OWN-WORLD: White ground. The act's stages as flat orienteering tints — olive, yellow, light
brown, light blue, pale violet — slanted bands with dashed boundaries. One purple, reserved for
the reader: the course, controls, the active role, links. Condensed sans for display and labels,
a calm reading face for text, tabular figures for dates and identifiers. Icon vocabulary:
start triangle, control circle, finish flag.

STORY: The founder sees the whole act as stages, picks a role, follows the course through the
articles that bind that role, sees when each part applies, and leaves knowing which articles to
read and when — with every line cited and the notices in view.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header — wordmark, the six first acts, search. Left five columns: breadcrumb,
title (quieter than the comp's), status line, plain-words opening, three actions, quick links.
Right seven columns: the process map with the course and the role box, as in the comp. Below:
your route, when each part applies, at a glance.

FORM: Orienteering map (dealt challenger, competitive), steered by the operator to an abstract
process map; seed:0a6d96979e429844.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
