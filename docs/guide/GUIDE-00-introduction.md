# 00 · Introduction

## What this is

EU Tech Reg is **an open map of EU technology regulation**, for people building AI systems and
robots in Europe and for the agents they work with.

You bring a question — *what applies to what we build, from when, and where does it say so* — and
the map shows you the acts that reach you, the articles to read for your role, and when each part
applies. Every fact on it links to the official text on EUR-Lex.

EU regulation is complex. The map exists so that people building in Europe do not give up in front
of it, and find what they need.

## Who it is for

**The founder of an AI or robotics startup.** The reference case. A small team putting an AI system
or a robot on the EU market, facing the AI Act, the Machinery Regulation, the Cyber Resilience Act,
the GDPR, the Data Act and product liability at once.

**The investor** asking what a company is exposed to and what lands in the next years.

**The company coming to the EU** from the US or elsewhere, asking which rules reach it from outside.

**The curious reader**, learning how the EU regulates technology.

**Their agents**, which consult the map over MCP and pass its citations on.

## Who it is not for

Stated plainly, because it shapes everything else:

- **Not for legal advice.** The map tells you what a text says, to whom, from when and where. It
  never tells you whether you are compliant or what you must do. That judgement is yours and your
  counsel's.
- **Not an official EU site.** EU Tech Reg is an independent project, not affiliated with the
  European Union.
- **Not for national law, yet.** Directives are transposed by each member state; the map covers the
  EU texts first.
- **Not a copy of the law.** It points to EUR-Lex; the official text is what counts.

## What is in it so far

The AI Act, as the first act, with its articles, its application dates after the Digital Omnibus
on AI, and the acts and guidelines beside it. The GDPR, Data Act, Machinery Regulation, Cyber
Resilience Act and Product Liability Directive follow, one at a time; until then they are marked
"Not yet" wherever they appear.
