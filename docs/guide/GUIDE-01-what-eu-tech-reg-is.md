# 01 · What EU Tech Reg is

## A map, drawn over the law

Each act is drawn as **terrain**: its stages from left to right — scope, banned practices, is it
high-risk, requirements, conformity, on the market. The terrain is the same for everyone; it is the
law's own structure, drawn by us.

Over it runs **your course**: a purple line from a start triangle, through numbered controls, to a
finish flag. Each control is an article to read for the role you chose. The route is ours — a
selection of where to start reading — not the act's, and the page says so.

## Built from records, cited at every step

Behind every page are records: the act, its articles, its dates, the acts around it. Each record
carries where it was read (an article on EUR-Lex), which act set it (the original or an amending
act), and the date it was read. The site and the MCP server read the same records, so a person and
an agent get the same facts.

## What it deliberately is not

| Not a… | Because… |
| --- | --- |
| Legal advice | It reports the text; it never assesses your situation. |
| Copy of EUR-Lex | It links to the official text and adds what EUR-Lex does not have: obligations by role, the dates that apply per part, one question across acts. |
| Chatbot | On the site you browse; for questions in words, you bring your own agent. |
| Official EU site | It is independent. |
| Complete | It says where it is not: acts not yet on the map, obligations not yet broken down, explanations not yet reviewed. |
