# eu-tech-reg

An open, structured map of EU technology regulation — AI, data, privacy, security, products and
machinery — that points to the official sources. For people learning it and for agents
consulting it (MCP from the start).

!!! warning "Not legal advice"
    Every fact here links to its official source and the date it was read; the official text is
    what counts.
