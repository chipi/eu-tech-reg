AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

Harmonised rules on artificial intelligence · in force since 1 August 2024 · amended by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744

The AI Act sets rules for AI systems sold or used in the EU, scaled to risk: some practices are banned, high-risk systems carry duties before and after they reach the market, and some systems must tell people they are AI.
