Words to know

Provider: whoever develops an AI system, or has one developed, and puts it on the market under their own name, paid or free. Our summary of Article 3(3). This route is drawn for a high-risk system.

Deployer: whoever uses an AI system under their own authority, other than for personal, non-professional use. Our summary of Article 3(4). This route is drawn for a high-risk system.

General-purpose AI model: a model that shows significant generality, can competently perform a wide range of distinct tasks and can be built into many downstream systems. Whoever develops one and puts it on the market under their name is its provider. Our summary of Article 3(63) and 3(3).

Whether a system is high-risk is a test the act sets out in Article 6 and Annex III; the map does not decide it. This short route starts with that test, then Article 50, which sets transparency duties for providers and deployers of certain AI systems.

About the act

Regulation · CELEX 32024R1689 · in force since 1 Aug 2024 · amended by Reg. (EU) 2026/1744 · 5 acts and guidelines beside it

The AI Act sets rules for AI systems sold or used in the EU, scaled to risk: some practices are banned, high-risk systems carry duties before and after they reach the market, and some systems must tell people they are AI.
