The mockup

Four screens to judge the approach before anything is built: structure first, then look. Every date, article title and identifier was read from the consolidated AI Act in Cellar on 14 November 2026; anything not read is labelled as such.

  • The AI Act

    The act as stages, your role as a course over them, the articles to read, when each part applies.

  • Your role, across every act

    One shared role, each act's own term beside it.

  • Search

    Filters by act, kind and binding force; the same index agents use.

  • What your agent gets back

    A cited MCP answer: provenance and a link to the map on every fact.

Not checked yet: that EUR-Lex pages use the same article anchors (#art_113) as the Cellar text. Obligations inside articles are not yet broken down, by design: that is pass 2.
