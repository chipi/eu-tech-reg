What you do, across every act

Start from what you do with the product. Each act gives that a legal name of its own: the AI Act says provider for whoever builds it and puts it out under their name, deployer for whoever uses it in their work. The page shows the act's word next to yours, so you can recognise it when you read the act. Grouping the acts' words under one plain question is EU Tech Reg's work, not the law's.
