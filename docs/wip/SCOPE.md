**Status:** candidate list (2026-11-14), for the operator to cut. Core vertical: AI and robotics
startups (operator, 2026-11-14); the list is deliberately wide.

## Coming from outside the EU — a view across the acts

Not more acts: questions a US or other non-EU company asks of the acts above, which the records
must be able to answer (operator, 2026-11-14). Which acts have these provisions, and where, is
unchecked.

- **Territorial reach** — does the act apply to a company with no EU establishment (for example
  because it offers to people in the EU, or its output is used in the EU)?
- **A presence in the EU** — must it name a representative or authorised representative in the
  EU (believed: GDPR, AI Act, Cyber Resilience Act, Digital Services Act, and for products the
  Market Surveillance Regulation's EU-based economic operator)?
- **Who carries the duty when it does not** — the importer or distributor in the EU, and what
  shifts to them.
- **Entry and timing** — what must be in place before the first sale, and what applies from the
  launch date.

## Not acts, but where the obligations become concrete

- **Delegated and implementing acts** under each act above.
- **Commission guidelines** (for example on the AI system definition and prohibited practices).
- **Codes of practice** (the general-purpose AI code of practice).
- **Harmonised standards** (CEN-CENELEC JTC 21 for the AI Act; EN ISO 10218 for industrial
  robots; ISO 13482 for personal care robots) — mostly paywalled, so pointed to, not described.

## Out, for now
